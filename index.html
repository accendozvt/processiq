Our 5-Step Process

From Discovery to Delivery

1
Week 1

Discovery Consultation

We begin with a complimentary discovery session to understand your business, goals, workflows, and the specific roles you need filled. We review your systems, software, security requirements, and performance expectations.

  • 30–60 minute video consultation with our team
  • Role specification, skills assessment, and volume planning
  • Security and data handling requirements review
2
Week 1–2

Proposal & Agreement

Within 48 hours of your consultation, we deliver a tailored proposal including candidate profiles, service scope, SLAs, pricing, and a proposed start timeline. We agree on KPIs, reporting cadence, and communication protocols.

  • Customised service agreement and SLA documentation
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs or lock-in penalties
  • Data security and confidentiality agreements (NDA/DPA)
3
Week 2–3

Talent Recruitment & Vetting

Our experienced HR team sources, screens, tests, and interviews candidates from our pre-qualified professional network in Kochi. Shortlisted candidates are presented to you for final approval before any placement is confirmed.

  • Skills testing, competency assessments, and background verification
  • Reference checks and professional qualification validation
  • Client interviews — you choose who joins your team
4
Week 3–4

Onboarding & Integration

Once your team is confirmed, we manage all onboarding logistics. Your new staff are set up on your software platforms, briefed on your brand standards, trained on your workflows, and introduced to your team.

  • Software access setup and security configuration
  • System and process training tailored to your workflows
  • Introduction to your team and communication protocols
5
Ongoing

Ongoing Management & Growth

After go-live, Process IQ Global continues to manage HR administration, performance monitoring, payroll, and compliance so you don't have to. We conduct regular check-ins and provide performance reports.

  • Monthly performance reporting and KPI tracking
  • Dedicated account manager for escalations and adjustments
  • Flexible scaling — add or adjust team members as your business grows

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get my team started?
From the initial discovery call to your first team member being operational typically takes 2–4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the role and your onboarding requirements.
What happens if a team member doesn't meet our expectations?
We include a performance guarantee. If any professional placed by Process IQ Global does not meet agreed performance standards within the first 30 days, we will replace them at no additional cost.
Do your staff work on our systems and software?
Yes. Our team can be trained to use your existing platforms — whether that's your CRM, accounting software, document management system, or project management tools. We do not require you to change your current technology stack.
How do you protect our data and confidential information?
We operate a highly secured environment with dedicated leased internet lines, 24/7 CCTV, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, and strict NDAs with all staff. No mobile phones or personal devices are allowed in the workplace. We can align our data protocols to meet GDPR, Australian Privacy Act, and other applicable frameworks.
Is there a minimum contract period?
We offer flexible engagement models starting from month-to-month arrangements. We believe our quality of service will retain clients — not lock-in clauses.
