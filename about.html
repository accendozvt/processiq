Our Story

A Different Kind of BPO Partner

Process IQ Global was founded in 2025 by a team of Australian and Indian business management specialists who saw a clear gap in the market: international companies needed more than a staffing agency, they needed a true offshore partner that understood their standards, their systems, and their ambitions.

Headquartered in Kochi, Kerala, one of India's most dynamic technology and shared-services cities, we combined deep knowledge of Australian, UK, and US business cultures with access to India's world-class professional talent pool.

The Kochi Advantage

Kochi is India's answer to Singapore, a cosmopolitan, well-connected city with world-class infrastructure and a rapidly maturing technology ecosystem. Home to Infopark and SmartCity campuses, Kochi is the preferred base for Australian and UK BPO operations in South Asia.

Stable modern IT infrastructure with high-speed connectivity
